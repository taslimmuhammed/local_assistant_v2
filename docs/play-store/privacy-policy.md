# Local Assistant: privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026. Developer: **Taslim Muhammed Moosa**, contact:
**developertaslim@gmail.com**.

Local Assistant is an AI assistant that runs on your phone. The AI model, your chats and what the
assistant remembers stay on your phone. There is no account, and no ads, analytics or tracking.
The developer receives nothing from the app unless you report a reply (see below), and never
sells data.

This policy says what the app reads, what it keeps on the phone, and the few times something
leaves the phone, always because you asked for it.

## What stays on your phone

The app reads these only to do what you ask, and keeps what it keeps in its own private storage
on the phone. None of it is sent to the developer or anyone else.

- **Chats and memory.** Your conversations, the facts you tell it to remember, notes, lists,
  reminders and events. They are excluded from Android's cloud backup and from device-to-device
  transfer.
- **Microphone.** Voice messages and voice questions. Speech is turned into text by your phone's
  own on-device speech recogniser, which does not send audio anywhere. If you turn on the wake
  word ("Hey Luna", off by default), a small model in the app listens for the phrase on the
  phone; what it hears is not kept or sent.
- **Contacts.** Read to find the person you name when you ask to call or message someone. The app
  opens your dialer or messaging app ready to send; it does not place calls or send messages
  itself.
- **Photos and videos.** If you turn on photo search, the app reads your photos to find them by
  what is in them, by place, and by the people in them (faces are matched against your own
  contacts' photos, on the phone). Where a photo was taken is matched against a list of place
  names stored in the app. You can share only some photos with the app if your Android version
  allows it.
- **Notifications.** If you turn on "While you were away" (off by default) and give the app
  notification access, it reads incoming notifications to summarise what you missed. These are
  kept for two days on the phone and then deleted. A reply is sent only after you confirm it.
- **What is on your screen.** When you open the assistant over another app, Android can give it
  a screenshot of that screen so you can ask about it. The screenshot is used for that question
  and discarded when the assistant opens again. It is never taken on the lock screen. You can
  turn this off in Android's digital assistant settings ("Use screenshot").
- **Health data.** If you allow it, the app reads steps, distance, calories, heart rate, resting
  heart rate, sleep and exercise from Health Connect, only when you ask about them. It only reads,
  never writes. The answer is worked out by the on-device model and stays in that chat, like any
  other answer. Health data is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared with anyone, and is
  not used to train any AI model.
- **Location.** If you set a place reminder ("remind me to buy milk near DMart"), the app uses your
  location to find "here" and nearby places, and, with "Allow all the time", watches for your
  arrival with the app closed. Location fixes come from Android and Google Play services' location
  service and are checked on the phone.

## When something leaves your phone

Only in these cases, and only for the thing you asked:

| What | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| The model and voice files the app downloads | Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and GitHub (github.com) | When you download the assistant model, turn on photo search, or download the natural voice. Like any download, these sites see your IP address. |
| Your web search, and your Tavily key | Tavily (tavily.com) | Only if you add your own Tavily key and ask something that needs the web. Tavily's privacy policy applies. |
| The name of a place you search for, and the rough area around you | OpenStreetMap's Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) | When you make a place reminder for a shop or address. Not your exact position. |
| The part of the map being shown | OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org) | When a place reminder's map is on screen, to load the map. |
| A reply you report: the reply, your message just before it, the reason and note you give, and the app's version | The developer, through Google Forms (Google is the form's host) | Only when you tap the flag under a reply and then Send report. Card numbers and similar details are hidden first. Nothing that identifies you or your phone is attached. |

Nothing else is sent. The app has no server of its own.

## Reports

Reports are kept in the developer's Google account, read only by the developer, and used only to
look into the reply and make the app's answers safer. They carry no name, account or device
identifier, so a report can't be traced back to you. Google, which hosts the form, sees the IP
address it came from, as with any web request.

## Your choices

- Every permission is asked for when a feature first needs it, and can be withdrawn in Android's
  settings at any time.
- Chats and what the assistant remembers can be deleted in the app. Clearing the app's storage in
  Android's settings, or uninstalling it, deletes everything it stored.
- The wake word, "While you were away", photo search, health data, web search and the natural
  voice are all off until you turn them on.

## Children

Local Assistant is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from
them. It collects nothing that identifies anyone.
