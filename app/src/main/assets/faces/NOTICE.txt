Face models used by photo search's People feature, from OpenCV Zoo (github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo).

yunet_2023mar.tflite
  YuNet face detector, face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx, by Shiqi Yu and contributors.
  MIT License (LICENSE-yunet.txt). Converted from ONNX to TensorFlow Lite with onnx2tf;
  weights and outputs unchanged.

sface_2021dec_int8w.tflite
  SFace face recognizer (MobileFaceNet trained with the SFace loss),
  face_recognition_sface_2021dec.onnx. Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE-sface.txt).
  Modified: converted from ONNX to TensorFlow Lite with onnx2tf, then its weights stored as
  8-bit integers (TensorFlow Lite dynamic-range quantization). Its outputs agree with the
  original's at a cosine similarity of 0.996 or more (tools/faces in this repository's history).
